## Status values

- `candidate`: researched but no preview built
- `preview-ready`: private preview passed QA
- `contacted`: first outreach sent
- `replied`: any reply received
- `interested`: asked for details or changes
- `won`: payment received
- `lost`: declined or no response after two follow-ups
- `do-not-contact`: opt-out, complaint, uncertainty, or unsuitable target
