FAQ

The details, upfront.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. Reviewing the preview costs nothing. Payment is due only if you choose to adopt and launch the website.

Will a website made for my business be published without permission?

No. Proposals are kept private and are not indexed by search engines or presented as your official website without approval.

What if the design is not right for me?

Tell us what you want changed. We can revise the copy, imagery, colors, and structure before launch without changing the package price.

Can I ask for the preview to be deleted?

Yes. Contact us and we will remove the relevant preview.

Who owns the finished website?

The delivered website can be used for your business. Domain, hosting, and handover details are explained clearly before launch.

Do I have to subscribe to a monthly care plan?

No. The JPY 98,000 website is a one-time purchase. SAKIMISE CARE at JPY 9,800 per month is available only if you want us to handle regular post-launch updates.
