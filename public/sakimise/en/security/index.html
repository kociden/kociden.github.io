SECURITY

Secure payments. Minimal data.

We do not hold card data

Card payments use Stripe-hosted Payment Links. SAKIMISE does not provide a custom card form and does not receive, store, or transmit card numbers or security codes.

Encrypted connections

Both the service website and Stripe Checkout use HTTPS. Customers should confirm that a payment link uses Stripe's official domain before entering payment information.

Reduced attack surface

The service website is static and has no customer accounts, login, uploads, database, or custom payment code. Customer data, payment data, and credentials are not placed in public directories.

Administration and review

Administrator accounts use multi-factor authentication. Malware protection is maintained, and source code and dependencies are reviewed regularly. A serious issue results in prompt remediation or temporary suspension.

Fraud controls

We rely on Stripe's card-testing controls, attempt limits, risk analysis, and 3D Secure when required. Suspicious payments are reviewed before production begins.

Report a concern

Please report a potential security issue privately to milk.lab28@gmail.com.
